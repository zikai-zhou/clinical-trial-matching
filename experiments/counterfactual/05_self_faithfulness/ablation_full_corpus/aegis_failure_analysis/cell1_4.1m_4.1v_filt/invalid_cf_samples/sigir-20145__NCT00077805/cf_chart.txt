A 56-year-old female on the 20th day post-left mastectomy presents to the emergency department complaining of shortness of breath and malaise. The patient says that she has remained in bed for the last two weeks. She reports new onset of right lower limb weakness and difficulty moving the leg, which has significantly impaired her ability to walk. The physical examination reveals tenderness on the left upper thoracic wall and right calf, as well as marked motor deficit in the right lower limb. The surgical incision shows no bleeding or signs of infection. Pulmonary auscultation is significant for bilateral decreased breath sounds, especially at the right base. Laboratory tests reveal an elevated D-dimer. The patient also describes sudden onset of slurred speech and right-sided weakness within the past 48 hours, consistent with acute ischemic stroke.
