A 14 year old male who fell on his outstretched left arm presents with pain and bruising on the inside and outside of the elbow, swelling, and inability to bend the arm. On the x-ray, the ulna has dislocated posteriorly from the trochlea of the humerus. The radius has dislocated from the capitulum of the humerus. There is an anterior dislocation of the shoulder joint. These injuries are not expected to affect the patient’s ability to participate in or benefit from a rehabilitative program.
