A 28-year-old female with neck and shoulder pain and left hand and arm paresthesias three months after returning from a trip to California where she attended a stray animal recovery campaign. Her physical exam was unremarkable except for slight tremors and almost imperceptible spasticity. She was prescribed NSAIDS and a topical muscle relaxant. She was brought in to the ER three days later with spastic arm movements, sweating, increasing agitation and anxiety, malaise, difficultly swallowing and marked hydrophobia, and was immediately hospitalized. Currently, she reports only intermittent slight tremors and near-imperceptible spasticity and otherwise appears free of obvious health problems.
