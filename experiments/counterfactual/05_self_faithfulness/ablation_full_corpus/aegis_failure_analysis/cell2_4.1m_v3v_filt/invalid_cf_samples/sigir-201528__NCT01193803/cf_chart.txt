A previously healthy 39-year-old man presents with a complaint of right lower extremity pain and fever. He reports limping for the past two days. The patient and his family report no previous trauma, but do remember a tick bite during a summer visit to Maryland several months ago. They do not remember observing erythema migrans. On examination, the right knee is tender and swollen. Peripheral WBC count and SRP are slightly elevated. Given the clinical suspicion for vertebral osteomyelitis in the context of infection, there is a need for spinal biopsy. No neurological deficits are reported on examination. Complaint is right lower extremity pain and limp; no spinal pain described. All clinical findings concern the right knee; no spinal findings present. Presentation involves knee, not spine (disc).
