A 62 yo male presents with four days of non-productive cough and one day of fever. He is on immunosuppressive medications, including prednisone. He is admitted to the hospital, and his work-up includes bronchoscopy with bronchoalveolar lavage (BAL). BAL fluid examination reveals owl's eye inclusion bodies in the nuclei of infection cells. However, there is no evidence of cytomegalovirus organ disease at this time, and the clinical findings do not strongly support a diagnosis of CMV organ involvement.
