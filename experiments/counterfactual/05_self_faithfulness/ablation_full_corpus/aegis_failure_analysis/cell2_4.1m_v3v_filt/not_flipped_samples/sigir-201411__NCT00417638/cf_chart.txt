A 40-year-old woman with no past medical history presents to the ER with excruciating pain in her right arm that had started 1 hour prior to her admission. She denies trauma. On further questioning, she reports a sudden onset of severe pressure and discomfort in the central chest, radiating to her right arm, which began about an hour before arrival. On examination she is pale and in moderate discomfort, as well as tachypneic and tachycardic. Her body temperature is normal and her blood pressure is 80/60. Her right arm has no discoloration or movement limitation. An electrocardiogram performed in the ER shows evidence of ongoing acute anterior myocardial infarction.
